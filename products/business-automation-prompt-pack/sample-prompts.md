# Sample Prompts - Business AI Automation Pack

## Category: Email Sequences

**Prompt 1: Cold Outreach Follow-up Sequence**
Act as a professional B2B sales copywriter. Write a 3-email follow-up sequence for a prospect who downloaded a free resource but hasn't responded. Keep tone helpful, not pushy. Include specific value in each email and a soft CTA. Target: [insert industry]. Product/service: [insert].

**Prompt 2: Newsletter Onboarding**
Create a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to a [niche] newsletter. Focus on delivering quick wins, building trust, and leading to a low-ticket offer. Include subject lines.

## Category: Content Outlines

**Prompt 3: LinkedIn Post Series**
Generate 10 LinkedIn post ideas and outlines for a [role] in [industry] that demonstrate expertise and drive engagement. Mix educational, story-based, and question formats.

## Category: Data Analysis

**Prompt 4: Sales Data Summary**
You are a business analyst. Given the following sales data summary [paste data], identify the top 3 insights, potential issues, and 2 recommended actions. Present in bullet points with supporting numbers.

## Category: Customer Support

**Prompt 5: Response Templates**
Create 8 professional customer support response templates for common issues in an e-commerce business: delayed shipping, product defect, refund request, size question, etc. Keep empathetic and solution-oriented.
